# Competitive diligence findings

**Bottom line:** Scientific-claim diligence is already marketed explicitly. The defensible opening is **quantum/deep-AI specialization plus demonstrated, leakage-controlled historical validation**, not merely “AI agents verify technical claims.”

**Evidence key:** F = full readable page; P = partial/JS-limited; S = search snippet only. Capabilities below are vendor claims, not independently exercised. “Not disclosed” means not found in reviewed sources.

### 1. V7 Go — direct positioning overlap

- **Workflow/customers:** Deep-tech VCs, diligence teams and R&D upload whitepapers, patents, papers and engineering documents; receive innovation summaries, patent-claim analyses, benchmarks, team backgrounds and technical-diligence reports. Advertises 30–60 minutes.
- **Independent verification:** More than summarization: competitor/industry benchmarking, academic credibility checks and prior-art search. However, patent databases **“can also be connected”**; risk detection explicitly scans documents for **“mentions of risks.”** This does not establish autonomous adversarial verification or physics-limit checking.
- **Evidence/commercial:** No technical-claim accuracy study, historical-company backtest or readable completed technical report found. General productivity testimonials are not validation. Custom annual pricing. V7 announced a **$33m Series A in November 2022**; current headcount unconfirmed.
- **Launch:** Go launched April 10, 2024; this agent’s launch is unverified. Shared October 2026 metadata is not reliable launch evidence.
- **Read:** [Agent](https://www.v7labs.com/agents/ai-venture-capital-technical-assessment-agent), [pricing](https://www.v7labs.com/pricing), [funding](https://www.v7labs.com/news/v7-raises-33m-series-a), [launch](https://www.v7labs.com/news/v7-go-why-we-built-it) — all F.

### 2. Startup Due Dil — broad claim-checking competitor

- **Workflow:** Early-stage VC/PE investors and founders upload decks/supporting files. Nine specialist agents plus “Oracle” produce cited red/yellow/green flags; users interrogate reports and rerun agents. Fund-thesis customization included.
- **Verification:** Explicitly compares submitted claims with public information. Technology agent covers defensibility, scalability and security; no demonstrated paper-level scientific verification or quantum expertise.
- **Evidence/commercial:** Creator manually refines missed nuances; admits mistakes remain. No quantified accuracy/backtest or public completed sample found. **$49/report; $449/month for 10; $999 for 25.**
- **Launch/size:** August 25, 2026; built by SMRK partner Vlad Tislenko for under €1,000, initially used internally/tested by investors. Funding/headcount not disclosed; predecessor GPT’s 10,000 users are **not this product’s users**.
- **Read:** [Site](https://startupduedil.com/), [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/08/25/inside-startup-due-dil-the-ai-tool-turning-hours-of-vc-diligence-into-10-minutes) — F.

### 3. WARP DD — strongest claimed backtesting overlap

- **Workflow:** Enter a technology theme; academic papers, patents, repositories and industry/startup data feed six-axis scores and PDF/PPTX reports for VC/PE/M&A. Ecosystem graphs and adoption forecasting, not demonstrated claim-by-claim rebuttal.
- **Validation:** Vendor release claims **89% accuracy**, 100 AI/ML companies, two-year windows; approximately 200 companies across seven domains. No disclosed dataset, leakage controls, baseline or reproducible methodology in reviewed sources.
- **Commercial/history:** Free five discoveries/month; paid **$29/$199/$599 monthly**, with 3/20/80 Deep-DD runs. Launched August 18, 2026; LINEdot founded May 2026. Funding/headcount undisclosed. No completed sample read.
- **Read:** [Release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/warp-dd-launches-ai-powered-technology-due-diligence-in-30-seconds-302854125.html), [pricing](https://warpdd.com/pricing.html) F; [app](https://warpdd.com/), [company product](https://www.linedotjp.com/warp-dd) P.

### 4. DeepDil.ai — primarily expert consultancy

Business questions/technology assets → six-dimension, five-level maturity assessment and execution roadmap for funds/PE. Covers architecture, AI readiness, security, team and defensibility. AI agents are explicitly mentioned for **SaaS delivery**, not proven diligence automation. Named Montefiore/Arche MC2 engagement and testimonials, but no scientific verification, backtest, sample, public pricing, launch, funding or headcount established. [Site](https://deepdil.ai/) F.

### Additional search: two strongest scientific matches

**Zetesis:** Deck/whitepaper → central hypothesis checked against **“primary scientific, clinical and regulatory sources”**; signed, referenced assessment, evidence-gap queries, optional “assess as of year.” Targets science/deep-tech investors; includes replication, IP and manufacturing. No public backtest, completed sample, pricing, launch or funding/headcount. [Home](https://zetesis.science/), [platform](https://zetesis.science/app/) F.

**Floatz:** Life-science investors/pharma/biotech; scientific agents, knowledge graph and living reports promise **“Claim-to-source referencing,”** evidence gaps and competitive therapeutic benchmarking. No public accuracy/backtest or confirmed launch/pricing/funding/headcount. [Home](https://floatz.ai/) P; [actual product client text](https://floatz.ai/_next/static/chunks/app/page-9c4d3df922796d66.js) F; [sample asset](https://terminal.floatz.ai/assets/mt-4561-prostate-cancer) P, login-blocked.
