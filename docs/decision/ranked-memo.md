**Decision, 2026-10-22 (late evening): build the Quantum Cryptanalysis Claim Ledger.** Its first release is a cited, narrow **ECC cost-comparison workbench**, not a quantum-computer resource estimator or a new leaderboard. [INFERENCE] Of the available options it has the clearest observable *repeat job* for a named potential user: Marin Ivezic already spends his own analysis on translating a passing point-addition score into a defensible risk claim; the ECDSA.Fail authors explicitly warn that different interfaces and accounting rules defeat direct dominance claims. ## Cross-check and scoring discipline

**Rubric:** nine columns in brief order: **U** evidence a *user for this artifact*, not just domain pain; **A** AI material to the job; **S** Severyn's advantage; **B** credible complete 12-hour demo; **J** investor-judge appeal; **P** post-Oct-25 adoption route; **C** competition openness (5 = open); **G** credibility in **both** AI and quantum; **R** low safety/1QBit risk (5 = low). Each is 1–5. Equal arithmetic totals are a screening aid; tie-break by U, then G, then J. **B≤2 disqualifies a weekend pick until a real constrained prototype resolves feasibility**; it does not erase the underlying opportunity from the ranking. No buyer confirmation means U ≤4. An impressive paper/program is not proof its readers will use a tool. Score a *single narrowed build*, not a union of proposed features. Scores are my [INFERENCE], not the reports' self-scores.

## Red team: what would kill the top three?

1. **Ledger — Eigen builds the same thing, while analysts prefer their own prose.** [HashSmash's design](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Layr-Labs/hash-smash-pareto/master/docs/HashSmash.md) already describes machine-readable sheets and cross-checked LLM cost estimates. [Ivezic](https://postquantum.com/security-pqc/ecdsa-fail-low-qubit-circuit/) is a possible competitor, not a committed buyer. Wrongly treating the 893-qubit point addition as a physical-machine or full-attack saving will damage trust, while the hosted ECDSA.Fail API terms preclude casual ingestion. **Cheapest deciding test before Saturday: yes**—send Ivezic (Applied Quantum/PostQuantum contact), Pierre-Luc Dallaire-Demers (Pauli Group; paper co-author) and Gautham Anant (Eigen Labs) a *one-page manually prepared side-by-side sample*, ask “Would you use this on the next claim, what line would you correct, and may I link your public submission?” Zero replies is not a negative result; one concrete correction or a next-claim request is stronger than praise. Do not wait for replies to start the build.
2. **QRef — most real ports are semantically correct; remaining failures are application-specific and cannot be automatically certified in 12 hours.** NVIDIA's agents eventually produced satisfactory ports *after human intervention*, and their own paper used broader suites and cross-agent judgment; a random demo could show only intentionally planted defects. Two pinned Qiskit runtimes, capture for `Sampler`/dynamic circuits and exact equivalence across representations make B=2. Vendor skills may internalize this. **Cheapest test before Saturday: yes**—port three actual legacy Qiskit examples with a coding agent, independently check reference observables/parameter sweeps and manually classify *unseeded* silent mismatches. If none, do not claim a measured X/6 agent failure rate or switch solely for a seeded demo. Ask QED-C's Thomas Lubinski whether a certificate of an actual problematic port helps their review.
3. **SRQC map — the named funded buyer cannot buy or adopt this stand-alone map by Sunday.** [DOE LAB 26-3603](https://science.osti.gov/-/media/grants/pdf/lab-announcements/2026/LAB-26-3603.pdf) allows only a lab to lead, LOIs have closed, and the whole-stack testbed needs real-hardware V&V; a few literature cards do not meet it. [INFERENCE] Experts will reject a forced conversion between Toffolis, logical operations and active volume. **Cheapest test before Saturday: outreach yes, definitive procurement no**—email program contact Dr. Marco Fornari or a known LAB 26-3603 team (ORNL, LBNL/NERSC) a *single quote-checked application card*, ask whether a **logical-only, non-converted** dataset would enter a proposal's benchmarking work. An emailed “yes, we would use it” is relevant; the lab's actual award decision is months away.

## Pick spec: ECC claim ledger (one-page build contract)

**User and job.** Marin Ivezic (Applied Quantum/PostQuantum.com) and Pauli Group's Pierre-Luc Dallaire-Demers compare fast-moving public ECC resource claims; Eigen's Gautham Anant is a possible maintainer/distribution partner, not a buyer. [INFERENCE] A PQC-risk analyst needs an evidence-backed explanation of **what exactly is counted, on what test support and at which abstraction layer**, before reciting a headline to a CISO. The MVP compares **two or three public secp256k1 point-addition claims only**; it makes no independent claim that any circuit is correct or attack deployable. Each card has source/version/date + quoted passage/page or table, point-addend interface (classical vs coherent), logical peak Q, Toffoli statistic, observed failures/sample/test selection, and explicit `not reported` for unknown fields. A comparison either lists *matched assumptions*, or states **not comparable** with the particular reason; no blanket “winner.”

**Two-minute live demo.** 0–20s: show an actual public claim that 893 logical qubits imply ~25% fewer **physical** qubits; quote its provenance without endorsing it. 20–60s: open the pre-curated 2026-10-13 circuit card and ECDSA.Fail paper comparator; highlight 893/Q, 2,218,127/T, and observed 111/360,960 failures with links. 60–90s: deterministic checker calculates ~0.000308 failure per sampled addition and `(1−111/360960)^9024≈0.062`; explain this is a plug-in probability estimate under **independent, stationary trials**, not a certificate; flag classical-vs-coherent addend and *no* published full-machine recomputation. 90–110s: paste a short quotation from another **supported** paper; LLM proposes a card; show source-span confirmation and a purposely unverified field withheld until approved. 110–120s: display field-level accuracy on a small **hand-labelled** held-out set (actual observed results only; never invent a pass rate) and link to code/demo. If live extraction is unstable, demonstrate the functioning seed-card reviewer, not a fake streamed result.

**Implementation/components.** Own schema, deterministic quote/string-span verifier and simple Python arithmetic; an LLM returns proposed JSON + text offsets from **allowlisted public paper text**, always reviewed before publication. SciPy [`scipy/scipy`](https://github.com/scipy/scipy), BSD-3-Clause, last pushed **2026-10-21** (GitHub API checked 10-22) for an **explicitly specified confidence interval**; [FastAPI](https://github.com/fastapi/fastapi), MIT, pushed **2026-10-22**, and [Gradio](https://github.com/gradio-app/gradio), Apache-2.0, pushed **2026-10-23 UTC** (API checked 10-22 ET), for one Render-hosted Python app. These are open-source dependencies, not employer assets. Public [arXiv 2609.09582v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09582), **CC BY 4.0**, cite and attribute; add only individually verified public paper snippets and a hand-authored seed set, not an unlicensed mirrored dataset or bulk copied PR bodies. Do **not** rely on `jieyilong/ecdsafail-supplemental-materials` until its data license is confirmed, or scrape ECDSA.Fail hosted API; request permission if needed. No circuit code, physical-QRE model, cryptanalytic optimization, or customer secrets.

**Data/compute/budget.** Six to eight evidence cards and six to ten *independent* manually labelled field examples are credible within 12 hours [INFERENCE]; a 20-paper auto-curated database is not. CPU only. [INFERENCE] At ~50–150k input tokens and ~5–15k output tokens total for this small corpus, Sonnet-class ~$3/$15 per million tokens implies about **$0.23–$0.68** in model fees before retries; reserve **$5–15** including development/re-extractions, not a promise that pending $100 credit has arrived. Budget cap and no API key in the browser. Host within the pending $50 Render credit if issued; otherwise use a public deploy that satisfies the live-link rule. Label demo data as frozen by date; do not advertise live monitoring.

**Prizes and trust boundary.** **Eon Era: strongest**, because field-level LLM extraction is checked against labelled truth, and failures are visible alongside the agent's trace. **SurrealDB Agent Memory: conditional**, not a cache labelled “memory”: if time permits, store versioned source→claim→assumption relations and a prior contradictory claim the extractor retrieves on the next extraction; [SurrealDB repo](https://github.com/surrealdb/surrealdb) exists, last push **2026-10-19**, GitHub API reports **NOASSERTION** license, so use the hosted service only under its service terms, not code reuse. **Clerk: weak**; analyst login doesn't make an anonymous public citation viewer better. **Expo: no fit**; a mobile app dilutes comparison legibility. Do not bolt on integration merely for prize coverage. Never use confidential 1QBit code, data, prompts or UI; stay strictly at source-attributed **logical** claim interpretation, no physical qubits/runtime, magic-state factories, lattice-surgery compiler, noise profiler or TopQAD-style estimates. Dual-use boundary: inspect already public claims but give no new attack construction or optimizations. Wording must distinguish *measured sample, extrapolation, and certified bound*; do not call the receipt a security certificate.

**12 actual hours (hour-by-hour, not feature creep):**
1. Choose two contrasting public ECC claims, preserve paper URLs/versions, write exact field labels and one honest non-comparability example.
2. Hand-label six cards with each numeric field linked to its actual quote; mark missing data unknown.
3. Hand-label 6–10 held-out fields and one deliberately ambiguous counterexample; freeze the evaluation set.
4. Implement source-span extractor interface and safe text-only paper ingestion (fixed allowlist; no arbitrary code/PDF execution).
5. Implement model JSON extraction and strict typed parsing; no ungrounded numbers go to published cards.
6. Add literal-span/units verification, `unknown` handling and source version/hash.
7. Add deterministic Q×T, sample-rate and independent-trial probability check; test with the known 111/360,960 case.
8. Add exact comparability gates (scope/interface/metric/corpus/failure); reject cross-layer physical conclusions.
9. Build two-card Gradio comparison with clickable quote and distinct observed/inferred labels.
10. Add held-out extraction evaluation panel with actual score and individual disagreements; polish a reviewable card.
11. Deploy public app and public repository, disclosure of dependencies/prior work and safety limits; freeze demonstration inputs.
12. Record ≤2-minute real demo, prepare Devpost write-up (stack, partner usage if real, security/ethics), verify linked quote labels and submit by Oct 25 11:59 PM ET.

**Cut order:** skip SurrealDB, Clerk, dynamic URL ingestion, chart, RSA, 20-paper corpus, automatic ongoing feed, confidence intervals if they cannot be defined correctly; *keep* two genuinely cited cards, source spans, a refusal to compare unlike quantities, one reproducible arithmetic check, working public demo, and labelled eval. If no external reply arrives, describe the person as a **target user**, never a validated customer.
