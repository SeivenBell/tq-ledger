## 3. The build, in one page

Full spec: `docs/build/PRODUCT.md`. Design: `docs/build/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**User**: the PQC risk analyst (e.g. Marin Ivezic) who must tell clients whether a new claim changes the threat timeline. **Buyer path**: analyst firms and in-house risk teams → CISOs, custodians, and insurers who need evidence for migration timelines → API for trackers and standards groups.

**What judges will see**

1. A home page that opens with the disagreement: public trackers say RSA-2048 needs 1,399, ~2,314, or ~4,000 logical qubits.
2. Card pages where every number opens its quote, location, arXiv version, and licence.
3. A compare page that refuses to compare logical with physical, one step with a full attack, or classical with coherent inputs, and names the reason; and that *does* compare when it's fair (for example, "34.5 % below Google's upper bound", where a headline said 50 %).
4. Recomputed checks: the 893-qubit circuit failed 111 of 360,960 tests; its whole 95 % interval [2.53, 3.70] × 10⁻⁴ is above another benchmark's 2 × 10⁻⁴ budget.
5. The AI loop: the model drafts a card, the verifier rejects a field whose quote isn't in the paper, and a reviewer approves the rest.
6. An eval page with real held-out numbers and a plain-chatbot baseline.

**Real findings already in hand (each recomputed on Oct 23)**

- Gidney 2025 prints "the expected number of shots is 9.2, giving an expected total time of 12.07 · 9.1/24 = 4.63 days". 12.07 × 9.1 / 24 = 4.58; 9.2 gives 4.63. A typo, conclusion unaffected: a good demonstration of the recompute check (present respectfully).
- Litinski's "only 50 million Toffoli gates" is a many-key limit; the single-key figure is 109 million.
- A co-author's "25 % fewer physical qubits" matches 1 − 893/1,175 = 24 %, a straight logical-to-physical scaling; Ivezic's architecture-aware re-derivation gives "16 % at most" for one platform.
- CoinDesk's "50 % cheaper" compares a classical-input average to Google's upper bound for a different circuit type; like for like it's 34.5 %.
