## Summary

**No reviewed product publicly demonstrated the complete proposed workflow:** claim-by-claim deep-tech verification, graded independent evidence, explicit technical/moat verdicts, and historical outcome backtesting. **GQI is the closest specialist competitor; AlphaSense and Hebbia are substantial horizontal substitutes.** Citation traceability is not proof that a technical claim is true.

Read depth: **F** = full substantive page; **P** = partial, truncated, or paywalled; **S** = snippet-only. Findings below describe advertised capabilities, not hands-on validation.

### 1. The Quantum Insider — specialist data plus AI Q&A

- **Inputs/outputs:** Announcements, filings, patents, papers, procurement and direct disclosures → company profiles, relationship graphs, comparisons, reports, API, and **“Natural-language AI queries over the entire dataset, with sourced answers.”**
- **“Analyst-verified” means:** Analysts check material records against their originating primary sources; ambiguous claims are flagged/excluded. The launch says records are checked **“against the document [they] came from.”** This establishes provenance, not independent experimental replication or physics-based falsification.
- **Customers:** Investors, enterprises, governments, academics; 200+ enterprise/government clients claimed.
- **Pricing:** Datasets **$2,999 named-user / $5,999 corporate**, one-time Excel + PDF; platform excluded. Platform annual, per-seat, quote-based; academic and enterprise subscriptions priced separately.
- **History/size:** Founded 2018; datasets launched **September 11, 2026**. Funding undisclosed in reviewed sources.
- **Evidence/samples:** Truncated workbook available on request; no accuracy benchmark/backtest found.
- **Read:** [Datasets P](https://thequantuminsider.com/data/), [launch F](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/09/11/the-quantum-insider-launches-datasets-analyst-verified-quantum-market-information/), [platform P, including searched pricing/methodology](https://thequantuminsider.com/platform/).

### 2. GQI — strongest technical-advisory overlap

- **Inputs/outputs:** Public/private/confidential quantum data, human/AI processing → technical benchmarks, analytics, expert reports and investor/startup diligence. Its **Quantum Tech Outlook Series 2026** is explicitly advertised.
- **Scorecards:** A readable portal index lists **qubit count, implementation, cloud platform, quality, technology, and software-startup partners**. This confirms technical comparison categories—not a demonstrated company-level evidence-graded verdict.
- **Independence:** Claims **“unbiased quantum tech business intelligence”** and proprietary expert vetting. No accessible sample established claim-by-claim contradiction handling or technical-claim grades.
- **Customers:** Vendors, users, governments, investors; Microsoft/AWS/Photonic/MITRE logos.
- **Pricing/history:** Launched December 2022; annual portal membership, price undisclosed. Separate QCR editorial membership **$100/year individual, $40 student**—**not established as full GQI access**. Funding/headcount unverified.
- **Accuracy:** No quantitative accuracy or outcome backtest found.
- **Read:** [home F](https://www.global-qi.com/), [intelligence F](https://www.global-qi.com/intelligence), [method F](https://www.global-qi.com/factory), [scorecard index F](https://my.gqifactory.com/), [Outlook P/paywall](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/global-quantum-ecosystem-analysis-gqis-2025-mid-year-outlook/), [launch F](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2022/12/200043-global-quantum-intelligence-a-business-intelligence-firm-launches-operations/).

### 3. AlphaSense — cited research and investment agents

500M+ documents, independent/broker research, expert interviews, filings and internal content → cited Q&A, competitive analyses, risk assessments and IC memos for VCs/financial enterprises. More than company-document summarization, but no demonstrated physics/code/benchmark claim adjudication. Accuracy evidence reviewed was qualitative testimonials, not a reproducible benchmark/backtest.

**Pricing:** Annual, quote-based **Market Intelligence / Enterprise Intelligence**; latter adds internal content; expert calls/models extra. Founded 2011; Deep Research operating by July 2025. June 2026 round: **$350M at $7.5B**, total funding >$1B; 8,000+ customers currently claimed.

**Read F:** [VC](https://www.alpha-sense.com/industries/venture-capital/), [pricing](https://www.alpha-sense.com/pricing/), [AI methodology](https://www.alpha-sense.com/resources/product-articles/ai-future-of-research/), [funding](https://www.alpha-sense.com/press/alphasense-raises-350m-at-7-5b-valuation-and-surpasses-600m-in-annual-recurring-revenue/).

### 4. Hebbia — configurable diligence infrastructure

Internal files, financial databases and web sources → Max reports/slides/models and Matrix cross-company analysis with **“complete traceability.”** Public financial demo includes confirmed/unconfirmed distinctions and risks; not deep-tech scientific validation. Targets investors, banks, lawyers, corporations. No accuracy/backtest demonstrated. **Pricing unavailable:** both pricing domains returned Contentful failure. **$130M Series B, July 2024**; already deployed then; exact launch unverified.

**Read:** [home P](https://www.hebbia.com/), [product P](https://www.hebbia.com/product), [funding F](https://www.hebbia.com/blog/hebbia-raises-usd130m-series-b), [pricing P/failed](https://www.hebbia.com/pricing).

### 5. Harmonic — sourcing, not scientific diligence

Startup/person/legal-filing data plus user rubrics/decks → Scout research briefs, competitor maps, ranked opportunities and scheduled monitoring. Claims discovery **“6–12 months ahead”**; no published validation/backtest found. VCs: 1,000+ investors claimed. **Console/API/Bulk**, all quote-based. Operating by November 2022; then **$23M Series A + prior $7M seed**. Scout launch date unverified.

**Read F:** [VC](https://harmonic.ai/solutions/vc) (P), [Scout](https://harmonic.ai/scout), [pricing](https://harmonic.ai/pricing), [2022 funding](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/07/harmonic-helps-investors-query-the-startup-searches-of-their-wildest-dreams/).

**Verdict — specialists:** GQI **3/5**; TQI **2.5/5**—expert intelligence, but no demonstrated backtested claim-verdict engine.  
**Verdict — horizontal substitutes:** AlphaSense **2.5/5**; Hebbia **2.5/5**—capable research infrastructure, not demonstrated scientific adjudicators.  
**Verdict — sourcing:** Harmonic **1.5/5**; differentiation remains plausible in independently graded technical falsification and leakage-controlled historical backtests.
