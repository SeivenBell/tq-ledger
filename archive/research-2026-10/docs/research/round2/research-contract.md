## Decision to make

Names and cosmetic feature differences do not count as distinct variants. An upstream contribution or reproducible technical artifact may beat a new stand-alone app.

## Compare every viable variant on the same evidence

| Question | Required evidence | What does not establish it |
|---|---|---|
| Who actually has this problem? | Named first-party request, workflow, issue or observed artifact; date and recurrence | Market size, job title, broad survey enthusiasm |
| What do they do now? | Existing tools exercised or read to the relevant feature; manual workaround | Search-result absence, assuming the incumbent cannot add a field |
| What specifically remains unmet? | A task/current input that the existing path cannot adequately complete | Another name, UI polish, quote links alone |
| Can we deliver it completely? | Public licensed input, objective observable/reference, a small exercised path, deployment/review budget | A list of individually feasible components |
| What does AI add? | A bounded transformation or diagnosis that a plain script/ordinary coding agent does not already provide as well | Merely writing the summary or inserting a chat box |
| How could it get adopted? | Integration/contribution route or a practitioner's actual proposed use | A message sent, a polite reply, a program's funding announcement |
| What could falsify the case? | Strongest competitor, failure boundary, simple deciding test | A one-line caveat followed by an unchanged build recommendation |

## Candidate families, not final products

- Quantum code reproduction/migration: independent behavioural referee vs end-to-end artifact revival vs upstream contribution.
- Scientific/resource claims: analyst comparison workbench vs evidence feed/correction contribution vs actual circuit re-grading.
- Adjacent computational research reproduction: environment execution is already served; look for a verified missing scientific-result workflow.
- Adjacent scientific operations: data handoff, units, parameter sweeps and failure recovery only where real requests and public inputs exist.

Variants must identify input, output, current alternative, decisive observable, and what is explicitly outside coverage. Broad 'prove correctness' or 'certify science' claims are not acceptable.
