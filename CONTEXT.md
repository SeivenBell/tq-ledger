# Quantum migration review

QRef reviews a declared quantum-code migration. Its language separates the model's proposal, observed execution, checker results and human judgment.

## Programs and scope

**Reference**:
The inspected implementation used as the preservation baseline. It can contain a bug and is not a scientific truth oracle.
_Avoid_: ground truth, correct version

**Target**:
The inspected implementation being compared with the reference. Being newer or generated by a model does not make it correct.
_Avoid_: fixed version

**Proposal**:
Model-generated target code and its explanation before independent execution and human review. A stated intention is not evidence that the code implements it.

**Case**:
A declared experiment contract: programs, inputs and the interpretation being reviewed. A case defines what its comparisons mean and what they exclude.

**Execution**:
One invocation of an implementation for one declared graph, angle setting and requested shot count.

**Submitted circuit**:
The circuit actually supplied to the simulator during an execution. A separately reconstructed circuit is not a substitute for this evidence.

**Circuit pair**:
The reference and target submitted circuits being compared under the same declared input and mapping assumptions.

**Shot**:
One simulated measurement. Requested shots and completed shots are distinct observations.

**Coverage**:
The declared questions and inputs that the record actually checked, left unchecked or could not support. Completion of declared cases is not whole-program coverage.

## Evidence and conclusions

**Observation**:
A value captured from a particular execution, including its inputs and provenance. It is distinct from a model's explanation of that value.

**Check**:
A comparison with an explicit method, criterion, scope and outcome. An inconclusive or unsupported check is not a successful check or proof of a defect.

**Difference**:
An observed mismatch under a stated comparison. It may be an intentional correction rather than an error.
_Avoid_: bug, failure, unless the evidence establishes that meaning

**Finding**:
A grouped explanation of related check outcomes. Repeated observations of one changed setting are not separate independent bugs.

**Evidence record**:
The immutable execution observations, check results, provenance and limits of a run. Human acceptance does not alter this record.

**Record identity**:
The identity of exact evidence bytes. A matching digest establishes consistency with those bytes, not authenticity or independent attestation.

**Review decision**:
A human's recorded disposition of a finding, tied to the exact evidence record and accompanied by a reason. The reviewer identity is self-reported, not authenticated.
_Avoid_: certification, automatic approval

**Report**:
A readable presentation of an evidence record and any applicable review decisions. It represents recorded work, not a live execution service.
